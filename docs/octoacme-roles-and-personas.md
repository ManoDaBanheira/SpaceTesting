# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### How They Interact
- **With Product Managers:** Collaborate on acceptance criteria and feature specifications
- **With Project Managers:** Participate in planning, estimation, and risk identification
- **With QA/Testing Leads:** Work together on test plans and quality standards
- **With Technical Leads:** Follow architectural guidance and participate in design reviews
- **With UX/Design Leads:** Implement designs and collaborate on usability feedback

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### How They Interact
- **With Project Managers:** Align on roadmap and priorities; sync on delivery timeline
- **With Developers:** Refine acceptance criteria and validate implementations
- **With QA/Testing Leads:** Define quality expectations and success metrics
- **With Stakeholders/Sponsors:** Communicate progress, gather feedback, validate outcomes
- **With UX/Design Leads:** Collaborate on user research and design validation

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### How They Interact
- **With Product Managers:** Align on priorities and delivery roadmap
- **With Developers:** Manage schedules, identify blockers, track progress
- **With QA/Testing Leads:** Coordinate testing timelines and release readiness
- **With Technical Leads:** Understand technical risks and dependencies
- **With Stakeholders/Sponsors:** Escalate issues, report progress, manage expectations
- **With Scrum Masters/Agile Coaches:** Coordinate ceremonies and team processes

---

## QA/Testing Leads

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads ensure quality standards are met throughout development and delivery. They own the testing strategy, coordinate QA activities, and validate that acceptance criteria are met before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and approach for the project
- Collaborate on acceptance criteria during planning
- Plan and execute manual testing, automation, and integration testing
- Identify quality risks and blockers
- Validate releases meet quality gates before deployment

### Goals
- Ensure reliable, high-quality deliverables
- Catch defects early and reduce production issues
- Provide confidence in release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Weekly QA sync and sprint planning
- Test plans and defect tracking
- Release readiness reports

### How They Interact
- **With Developers:** Collaborate on test plans, review code for testability, validate fixes
- **With Product Managers:** Align on quality expectations and acceptance criteria
- **With Project Managers:** Report testing progress, escalate quality risks
- **With Technical Leads:** Understand architectural implications for testing
- **With UX/Design Leads:** Validate user experience and usability requirements
- **With Stakeholders/Sponsors:** Report release readiness and quality metrics

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide business context, approve major decisions, and ensure project outcomes align with organizational goals. They champion the project and secure resources.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria
- Approve scope, timeline, and resource decisions
- Escalate blockers and resolve cross-functional dependencies
- Communicate project status to leadership
- Provide feedback and validate outcomes

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Maintain executive alignment and buy-in
- Mitigate business and organizational risks

### Typical Communication
- Monthly status updates and milestone reviews
- Escalation and decision forums
- Quarterly business reviews

### How They Interact
- **With Project Managers:** Receive escalations, approve major changes, provide strategic direction
- **With Product Managers:** Validate business objectives and measure impact
- **With Technical Leads:** Understand technical trade-offs and feasibility
- **With QA/Testing Leads:** Review quality and release readiness reports
- **With Developers:** May observe demos and provide feedback on deliverables

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical strategy, guide architectural decisions, and mentor the development team on best practices. They balance scalability, performance, and maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical architecture and design decisions
- Review technical approaches and code quality
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers and support skill development
- Ensure alignment with broader technical strategy

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, high-performance systems
- Reduce technical debt and improve system health
- Build team capability and knowledge sharing

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review guidance and mentorship
- Technical risk assessments and mitigation plans

### How They Interact
- **With Developers:** Provide architectural guidance, conduct design reviews, mentor on technical practices
- **With Project Managers:** Communicate technical risks and dependencies, inform timeline estimates
- **With QA/Testing Leads:** Discuss testability, performance testing, and technical test strategies
- **With UX/Design Leads:** Advise on technical feasibility of design requirements
- **With Stakeholders/Sponsors:** Communicate technical trade-offs impacting business decisions

---

## UX/Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads ensure that solutions are user-centered, intuitive, and aligned with product vision. They collaborate with product and engineering to validate design decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and define design requirements
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Validate design solutions with users and stakeholders
- Collaborate on acceptance criteria related to user experience
- Ensure consistency across user interfaces and experiences

### Goals
- Maximize user satisfaction and usability
- Reduce support burden through intuitive design
- Accelerate user adoption and engagement

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and user testing sessions
- Specifications and design system documentation
- Product and engineering collaboration meetings

### How They Interact
- **With Product Managers:** Conduct user research, validate design concepts, measure usability
- **With Developers:** Collaborate on implementation, provide detailed design specs, review implementations
- **With QA/Testing Leads:** Validate user experience acceptance criteria, coordinate usability testing
- **With Technical Leads:** Ensure design feasibility, understand technical constraints
- **With Stakeholders/Sponsors:** Present design concepts, gather stakeholder feedback, demonstrate user value

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate the adoption of Agile practices, manage team ceremonies, and help the team continuously improve their delivery processes.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, sprint reviews, and retrospectives
- Help remove impediments and blockers
- Coach the team on Agile principles and practices
- Track team velocity and identify improvement opportunities
- Foster psychological safety and encourage collaboration

### Goals
- Enable smooth, predictable delivery cadence
- Build a high-performing, self-organizing team
- Continuously improve processes and team practices

### Typical Communication
- Facilitating team ceremonies and retrospectives
- Coaching conversations with team members
- Process improvement recommendations and action items

### How They Interact
- **With Project Managers:** Support project execution and ceremony coordination
- **With Developers:** Coach on Agile practices, help remove blockers, foster collaboration
- **With Product Managers:** Ensure backlog is well-refined and priorities are clear
- **With QA/Testing Leads:** Ensure testing is integrated into sprint workflows
- **With All Roles:** Foster team collaboration and psychological safety
